Our website

Privacy, in plain terms.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Di Dee Designers ("we", "us") runs this website, dideedesigners.in, so you can learn about our saree pre-pleating and box-folding service in Tiruppur, Avinashi and Erode and contact us directly. This page explains, in plain terms, what information this website handles.

What this website itself collects

This website has no contact forms, no accounts, no comment boxes and no newsletter signups, and its pages contain no analytics or tracking code. The website itself does not gather personal information from your visit.

Hosting

Like most websites, this site is served by a hosting provider (GitHub Pages), which handles the standard technical information needed to deliver web pages to your browser. GitHub's own privacy policy applies to that processing.

When you contact us

If you message us on WhatsApp (+91 90030 37820), you share your phone number and whatever details you choose to send, such as your location, occasion date, saree type and quantity. WhatsApp and Instagram are run by their own companies, and their own privacy policies apply to anything you share with us there.

Questions about your details

If you have a question or a request about the details you have shared with us, message us on WhatsApp and we will help.

Changes to this page

If we change how this website handles information, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Message us on WhatsApp or through our Instagram page. Di Dee Designers, Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, India.
