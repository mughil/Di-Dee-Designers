Common questions

FAQ

Do you sell sarees?

No. We prepare the sarees you already own. Whether it is a workday, a wedding or a family function, we pleat and box-fold your own sarees; we do not sell them.

How much does it cost?

Box Folding is ₹250, Semi-Fluffy Pleats are ₹300 and Full-Fluffy Pleats with Hanger are ₹400. Tell us your saree type and quantity on WhatsApp and we will confirm the price before you book.

Which areas do you serve?

We work across Tiruppur, Avinashi and Erode. Scheduled pickup and doorstep delivery depend on your area and date, so share your location when you message us.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp with your location, occasion date, saree type and quantity, plus the style you like and when you need the saree. We confirm the price and arrangements before you book.

Can you handle silk or bridal sarees?

Yes. Premium silk, bridal and wedding sarees get personal attention, and we handle heavy and delicate fabrics with care.

What if I need my saree urgently?

Tell us the date and time you need it. Express preparation depends on availability, and we will confirm what is possible before you book.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

Yes. You can drop off your saree or set up a scheduled pickup, and we offer doorstep delivery on our routes in Tiruppur, Avinashi and Erode. What we can do depends on your area and date.

Do you take family or bulk orders?

Yes. Bring several sarees together for a family function or wedding. We also have packages for makeup artists, parlours, saree shops and boutiques. Send the quantity and dates and we will quote.
