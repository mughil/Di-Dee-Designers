What is saree pre-pleating?

Saree pre-pleating is a service where your own saree is pleated and box-folded in advance, so you can wear it in a few minutes without arranging pleats by hand. Di Dee Designers in Tiruppur pre-pleats cotton, silk, bridal and delicate sarees.

How much does saree pre-pleating cost in Tiruppur?

At Di Dee Designers in Tiruppur, box folding (pressed pleats) costs ₹250, semi-fluffy pleats cost ₹300 and full-fluffy pleats with a hanger cost ₹400, per saree. The final price is confirmed on WhatsApp before you book.

What is the difference between box folding, semi-fluffy and full-fluffy pleats?

Box folding gives flat, even, pressed pleats folded into a box shape, suited to everyday and office sarees. Semi-fluffy pleats are soft and rounded with moderate volume, suited to functions and festivals. Full-fluffy pleats have full volume and come on a hanger, suited to weddings and bridal sarees.
