Terms of service.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

These terms apply to the dideedesigners.in website and to booking our saree preparation service. They are written to be clear, not legal-heavy. If anything is unclear, ask us on WhatsApp before you book.

What we do

We pre-pleat and box-fold sarees you already own, and we give silk, bridal and delicate fabrics personal care. We prepare sarees; we do not sell them.

Prices

Prices shown on this website (Box Folding ₹250, Semi-Fluffy Pleats ₹300, Full-Fluffy Pleats with Hanger ₹400) are our listed prices. The final price for your order, including any family, bulk or business package, is confirmed on WhatsApp before you book.

Booking, pickup and delivery

Bookings are made by messaging us on WhatsApp with your location, occasion date, saree type and quantity. Scheduled pickup and doorstep delivery run on our routes in Tiruppur, Avinashi and Erode, and what we can arrange depends on your area and date. Express preparation depends on availability; we confirm what is possible before you book.

Your sarees

Please tell us about anything special when you book: delicate fabric, heavy work, or a style you have in mind.

Contact
