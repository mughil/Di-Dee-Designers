Saree Pre-Pleating in Tiruppur

Saree pre-pleating means preparing the pleats of a saree in advance, so they are already arranged when it is time to wear it. Di Dee Designers pre-pleats your own saree in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu. We prepare sarees; we do not sell them.

About our pre-pleating service

Skip the pleating. Dress with ease. Instead of arranging pleats by hand, choose the pleat style you want for your own saree. Three pleating options are available, each priced per saree.

Semi-fluffy pleats

₹300 per saree

Full-fluffy pleats with hanger
