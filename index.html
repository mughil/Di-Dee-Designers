What is saree pre-pleating?

Saree pre-pleating is a service where your own saree is pleated and box-folded in advance, so you can wear it quickly without arranging pleats by hand. Di Dee Designers in Tiruppur pre-pleats silk, bridal and delicate sarees with personal care, with scheduled pickup and delivery in Tiruppur, Avinashi and Erode. Prices start at Rs 250 per saree, and we confirm the final price on WhatsApp.

Saree pleating terms explained

Box folding
Neatly folding the finished pleats into a flat box shape so the saree stays ready to wear.
Pressed pleats
Flat, even pleats set by pressing, ideal for everyday wear.
Semi-fluffy pleats
Pleats with moderate volume for a fuller, softer look at functions.
Full-fluffy pleats
Full-volume pleats, delivered on a hanger, for weddings and bridal sarees.
