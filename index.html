Saree pre-pleating FAQ.

Quick answers about prices, areas, fabrics and booking. See all questions

What is saree pre-pleating?

Saree pre-pleating is a service where your own saree is pleated and box-folded in advance, so you can wear it in a few minutes without arranging pleats by hand. Share your saree details before booking so preparation suitability can be confirmed.

How much does saree pre-pleating cost in Tiruppur?

At Di Dee Designers in Tiruppur, box folding (pressed pleats) costs ₹250, semi-fluffy pleats cost ₹300 and full-fluffy pleats with a hanger cost ₹400, per saree. The final price is confirmed on WhatsApp before you book.

Which areas does Di Dee Designers serve?

Di Dee Designers lists Tiruppur, Avinashi and Erode in Tamil Nadu as service areas. Contact us with your location and required date to confirm whether your request can be accommodated and what handover arrangements are available.

Can silk, bridal or delicate sarees be prepared?

Share the fabric, embellishments and condition of your saree before booking. We will confirm whether the requested preparation is suitable.

Can you prepare a saree for an urgent date?

Share the date you need your saree before booking. We will confirm whether the requested preparation can be completed by then. An urgent or express slot is not guaranteed until confirmed.

Can I enquire about more than one saree?

Send the quantity, saree details and required date on WhatsApp. We will confirm whether the request can be accommodated and provide any applicable price before booking.
