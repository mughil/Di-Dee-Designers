Privacy policy

How we handle your information.

Who we are

Di Dee Designers ("we", "us") runs this website, dideedesigners.in, so you can learn about our saree pre-pleating and box-folding service in Tiruppur, Avinashi and Erode, Tamil Nadu.

What this website collects

This website has no sign-up, login, contact form or advertising trackers, and it does not set cookies. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address and browser type, to run and secure the site.

When you contact us

If you message us on WhatsApp, call us, or reach us on Instagram, we receive the details you share, such as your name, phone number, area, occasion date and saree details. We use them only to give you a price, arrange preparation, pickup and delivery, and follow up on your order. Those apps also handle your messages under their own privacy policies.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only when needed to complete your order, for example with a delivery person on our route, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep order conversations only as long as we need them to serve you and keep basic business records.

Your choices
